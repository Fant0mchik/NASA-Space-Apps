                   BROWSE Directory Contents

The BROWSE directory contains colorized representations of the thermal 
inertia maps.  A large (approximately 3.3 Mb) and small (approximately 
0.15 Mb) version is available for both nightside and dayside thermal 
inertia maps. In filenames "d" denotes dayside maps, "n" denotes nightside
maps, "b" stands for bolometer, and "s" stands for small. Browse images
have a west positive longitude label of 0-360 degrees.  Units for the 
browse images are tiu (tiu = J/(m^2*K*sqrt(s)) ).


dbmap2007.jpg -   3.3 Mb jpeg browse image of the daytime thermal inertia
                  map (global_ti_day_2007)

dbmap2007s.jpg -  149 Kb jpeg browse image of the daytime thermal inertia
                  map (global_ti_day_2007)

nbmap2007.jpg -   2.85 Mb jpeg browse image of the nighttime thermal 
                  inertia map (global_ti_night_2007)

nbmap2007s.jpg -  146 Kb jpeg browse image of the nighttime thermal 
                  inertia map (global_ti_night_2007)
